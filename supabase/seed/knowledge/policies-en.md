# Avenlo Shipping, Returns, and Warranty Policy

This policy is for the GroundDesk Demo Workspace. Every order is simulated and generated relative to the date on which the demo data is initialized.

## Shipping estimates

In-stock orders are normally processed within one to two business days. Standard delivery is estimated at three to seven business days after dispatch, while expedited delivery is estimated at one to three business days. Cross-border destinations, remote areas, customs checks, severe weather, and carrier disruptions can add delays; an estimate is not a delivery guarantee.

For pending or processing orders, support may explain the current state but must not promise an exact dispatch time. In-transit orders should rely on the redacted carrier status returned by the order tool.

## Standard return window

Standard returnable products may be requested for return within 30 days after delivery. In-ear Airwave S1 and Airwave S1 Pro products use a 14-day sealed-product window. Opened in-ear products cannot be auto-approved by AI and require manual review. An opened Orbit H5 also requires a manual condition and accessory review.

Shipping damage must be reported within seven days of delivery, with packaging and damage photos retained. A wrong-item case goes to manual review, and the customer is not expected to pay return shipping. Reserve 10K power banks must use the designated Avenlo ground-return label.

An eligible result only permits creation of a request pending human approval. It does not mean a refund, replacement, or order change has occurred. GroundDesk AI and its tools cannot issue refunds.

## Limited warranty

Earphones, headphones, chargers, power banks, and Dockline 6 have a 24-month limited warranty from the original purchase date. LinkCore C240 cables have an 18-month limited warranty. Coverage applies to defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. It excludes loss, liquid exposure, accidental damage, unauthorized repair, normal wear, and damage caused by power sources outside specification.

A warranty request needs an order number, the matching purchase email, a fault description, and potentially photos or video. AI may explain the policy and create a human ticket but cannot promise an immediate replacement.

## Batteries and air travel

Reserve 10K is approximately 38.5 Wh and must be carried in cabin baggage, never checked luggage. A damaged, swollen, or recalled battery must not be shipped or taken aboard; stop using it and request human support. Airlines and regions may impose stricter rules, so GroundDesk must not replace an airline's final decision when the documentation is insufficient.
