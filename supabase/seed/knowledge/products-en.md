# Avenlo Product and Compatibility Guide

This document belongs to the fictional Avenlo merchant used by the GroundDesk demo. Every product, price, and specification is simulated and is not a real brand or transaction.

## Airwave S1 True Wireless Earbuds

Airwave S1 uses Bluetooth 5.3 and provides dual-microphone call noise reduction. It does not have active noise cancellation and does not support simultaneous connections to two devices. Combined listening time with the case is about 28 hours, and the earbuds are rated IPX4. Airwave S1 uses the S1-only charging case.

## Airwave S1 Pro ANC Earbuds

Airwave S1 Pro uses Bluetooth 5.4 and supports adaptive active noise cancellation plus two-device multipoint. Combined listening time with the case is about 36 hours, and the earbuds are rated IPX5. The S1 Pro case is not compatible with the standard Airwave S1 even though the models look similar.

## Orbit H5 Over-Ear ANC Headphones

Orbit H5 supports hybrid ANC, Bluetooth 5.4, two-device multipoint, and up to about 55 hours of listening. Wired listening works through USB-C digital audio or a 3.5 mm input. Current documentation does not state whether Orbit H5 supports spatial audio or console-specific gaming protocols.

## Flux 67 and Flux 100 GaN Chargers

Flux 67 has two USB-C ports and one USB-A port. A single port can supply up to 67 W, while simultaneous use dynamically shares power. It supports PD 3.0, PPS, and QC 4+. Flux 100 has three USB-C ports and one USB-A port; USB-C1 can supply up to 100 W and supports PD 3.1, PPS, and QC 4+. Actual output depends on device negotiation.

## LinkCore C240 Cable

LinkCore C240 is a 2 m braided USB-C to USB-C cable with an E-Marker and up to 240 W EPR charging. Data is limited to USB 2.0 at 480 Mbps. It does not support DisplayPort Alt Mode or any video output.

## Reserve 10K Power Bank

Reserve 10K has a nominal 10,000 mAh capacity, approximately 38.5 Wh, and up to 30 W bidirectional USB-C. It must travel in cabin baggage and must not be placed in checked luggage. Airline and departure-country rules still apply.

## Dockline 6 Desktop Charging Dock

Dockline 6 provides four USB-C and two USB-A ports with 200 W total output. It can power up to three laptops alongside mobile devices. Its front display shows power status only; this product is not a video or data dock.
